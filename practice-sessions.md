# Practice Sessions

When you start a session, the app begins tracking your time and suggests tunes that need practice. This helps you focus on what matters most instead of wondering what to practice next. At the end of each session, you can see how much you accomplished, which builds motivation and helps you track your progress over time.

## Starting a Practice Session

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Navigate to Practice tab</strong>
    <p>Tap the play button icon in the bottom navigation bar.</p>
</div>


<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Tap "Start Practice Session"</strong>
    <p>This begins a new practice session and starts the session timer.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <strong>Select an instrument (optional)</strong>
    <p>If instrument tracking is enabled in Settings, you'll be prompted to select which instrument you're practicing with.</p>
</div>

## Selecting Tunes to Practice

To select a tune to practice, you start with the "Choose Tune to Practice" button on the main practice screen:

This brings up the tune picker. The tune picker includes several icons to help you find the right tune quickly:


- 🔍 **Search** - Search for tunes by title
- ❤️ **Favorites** - Show only your favorite tunes
- 📖 **Someday** - Show tunes marked as "Someday" (tunes you want to learn later)
- ⏰ **Due** - Show only tunes that are due for spaced repetition review
- 🔀 **Shuffle** - Pick a random tune from the current list
- ⇅ **Sort** - Change sort order (Name, Tradition, Comfort Level, or Key)
- ⚙️ **Filter** - Open advanced filters (Tradition, Key, Mode, Tags)
- 📑 **Saved Searches** - Access your saved searches

## Practice Tools

### Session Timer

The session timer automatically tracks how long you've been practicing. It starts when you begin a session and continues until you end the session.

### Metronome

The metronome provides a steady beat to help you practice at a consistent tempo. Use it when you're learning a new tune to keep yourself from rushing, or when you're polishing a tune to make sure you can play it at the right speed.

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Access the metronome</strong>
    <p>During a practice session, tap the metronome button.</p>
</div>


<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Start the metronome</strong>
    <p>Tap the play button to start the metronome at the current BPM setting.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <strong>Adjust tempo</strong>
    <p>Tap the settings icon to open metronome settings where you can adjust BPM, time signature, and sound options.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">4</span>
    <strong>Stop the metronome</strong>
    <p>Tap the pause button to stop the metronome.</p>
</div>

### Rest Timer

The rest timer helps you take breaks between practice sessions or tunes. Set it for 2-5 minutes when you feel yourself getting tired, or use it to structure your practice into focused blocks with breaks in between.

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Open rest timer</strong>
    <p>During a practice session, tap the rest timer button.</p>
</div>



<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Set duration</strong>
    <p>Choose a preset duration or set a custom time. The default is 2 minutes.</p>
</div>



<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <strong>Start the timer</strong>
    <p>Tap "Start" to begin the countdown. You'll receive a notification when the timer completes, even if the app is in the background.</p>
</div>


### Session Notes

**Why take notes:** Notes help you remember what you worked on and what to focus on next time. Maybe you discovered a tricky passage that needs more work, or you figured out a fingering that works well. Notes make it easy to pick up where you left off.

You can add notes during or after a practice session. Session notes are general notes about the whole practice session (like "worked on Irish tunes today" or "focused on tempo"). Tune-specific notes appear when you practice that tune again, so you can remember what you were working on last time.

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Add session notes</strong>
    <p>During a session, tap the notes field to add general notes about your practice - what you focused on, how it went, or what you want to work on next time.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Add tune-specific notes</strong>
    <p>When rating a tune, you can add notes specific to that tune. These notes will appear the next time you practice that tune, so you can remember what you were working on - like "work on the B section" or "fingering for measure 8 needs practice."</p>
</div>

## Rating Your Practice

After practicing a tune, you rate how it went. Ratings are how the app learns which tunes you have a grip on and which ones still need attention. Be honest - the more accurate your ratings, the better the app can help you.

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Finish practicing a tune</strong>
    <p>When you're done with a tune, you'll see the practice rating screen.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Select a rating</strong>
    <p>Choose one of three options:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Needs Work</strong> - You struggled with this tune or made mistakes</li>
        <li><strong>Almost</strong> - You played it reasonably well with minor issues</li>
        <li><strong>Got It</strong> - You played it confidently and accurately</li>
    </ul>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <strong>Write a "For next time" note (optional)</strong>
    <p>Jot down what you worked on, what was awkward, or what to pick up next time - like "work on the B section" or "fingering for measure 8." The note comes back to you the next time this tune turns up.</p>
    <p>You can also flip on <strong>Work on this</strong>. That puts the tune in the "To work on" pool, which is one of the lists you draw from when you plan your week.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">4</span>
    <strong>Save the rating</strong>
    <p>Tap "Save" to record your rating.</p>
</div>

<div class="info">
    <strong>How ratings move a tune's Comfort Level:</strong>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Needs Work</strong> → drops the tune one Comfort Level</li>
        <li><strong>Almost</strong> → holds where it is, and breaks any run of "Got It"s</li>
        <li><strong>Got It</strong> → three in a row move the tune up one Comfort Level</li>
    </ul>
    <p>There are no due dates to keep up with, and nothing goes overdue. Ratings
    shape your Comfort Levels; you decide what to practice when you plan your
    week. See <a href="/practice/spaced-repetition.html">Spaced Repetition</a>.</p>
</div>

## Ending a Practice Session

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Tap "End Session"</strong>
    <p>When you're done practicing, tap "End Session" in the practice view.</p>
</div>


<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Review session summary</strong>
    <p>You'll see a summary of your session including total time, tunes practiced, and any goals completed.</p>
</div>


<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <strong>Add final notes (optional)</strong>
    <p>You can add or edit session notes before saving.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">4</span>
    <strong>Save the session</strong>
    <p>Tap "Save Session" to record your practice. The session will appear in your practice history.</p>
</div>

## Canceling a Session

If you need to cancel a session without saving:

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Tap "Cancel Session"</strong>
    <p>This option is available during an active session.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Confirm cancellation</strong>
    <p>You'll be asked to confirm. Canceling will discard the session and any unsaved progress.</p>
</div>

<div class="warning">
    <strong>Note:</strong> Canceling a session will not save any practice time, tune ratings, or notes. Make sure you want to discard this session before confirming.
</div>
