Note down the tunes at a jam

Note down what got played without stopping.

What's said

1

At a jam, tunes go by faster than you can write them down, and nobody wants to be the one on their phone all night.

2

This is the whole thing: a page to jot on. One tune per line, as it gets called. Spelling doesn't matter, and it doesn't have to be a tune you have — you can add it afterwards.

3

So you type as you go.

4

If you've got a second, you can say more. A hash tag marks how it went.

5

And a line starting with a dash is a note about the tune above it — the thing you'll want next week and won't remember tonight.

6

When it's your turn to call one and your mind goes blank, you can pick out of your own library instead of typing.

7

Tap a tune and its name drops into the jam, and you're straight back where you were.

8

Then it's over, and you sort it out — on the drive home, or the next morning.

9

The app has matched what you typed against your library. Anything it placed says so underneath.

10

Anything it couldn't place is waiting for you. The arrows beside it let you point at the tune you meant, or add it as a new tune in your library.

11

Save it and the whole jam is logged.

12

And there it is in the journal, in with your practices. This helps you see which tunes you know well, and which you haven't played in a while.
