Add a recording or video to a tune

Put the version you're learning from on the tune.

What's said

1

You can keep track of recordings of a tune, from streaming services, and also video and audio recordings you make. This way you can find them quickly when you sit down to play.

2

You can record your own audio of playing a tune.

3

Play a few bars and stop.

4

Then you can listen back to what you played, and hear what went well, and what you can improve.

5

You've probably already got videos of this tune on your phone — from a lesson, or somebody playing it at a jam.

6

Media is where those go.

7

Pick the clip you want. You can take more than one.

8

Save, and it sits on the tune with everything else.

9

Links is for versions other people made. The search row looks the tune up on YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music and the rest — and when you find one you like, you can share it back to Practice! from there.

10

If you come home from a festival with a pile of clips, you don't have to do them one at a time.

11

Play each one, and tap the tune it belongs to.

12

So next time you sit down with this tune, your own playing and the versions you're learning from are both right there.
