Track a practice session

Walk through a practice, and see where it gets written down.

What's said

1

When you practice, you can keep track of what you've played, and leave notes about each tune for next time. Let's try it out.

2

Now you can choose what to work on. "Pick a Tune" opens your whole library.

3

The rows underneath are short cuts into different parts of it. Exercises is what you warm up on — scales, bowing drills, whatever you do first.

4

This Week is the tunes you've decided to focus on right now.

5

And Repertoire is the ones you know cold.

6

Whatever you tap here is what you're playing.

7

Here's the tune, and the note you left yourself the last time you played it. Now go play it.

8

You can pull up a metronome and set the tempo where you want it.

9

You can record a quick clip of yourself, to hear how it actually sounded.

10

Or open the tune's details, where the recordings and videos you've saved for it live.

11

When you're done, say how it went: Needs Work, Almost, or Got It. If you rate a tune Got It three times in a row, we add it to your Repertoire list.

12

Then leave yourself a note. You will see this next time you practice this tune.

13

And if it's something you want to come back to, flip "Work on this". When you plan for the week, you can see a list of all the tunes where you've selected this.

14

Tap Next to go on to the next tune.

15

Play as many as you like. When you've had enough for the day, finish up.

16

Here's the whole session — what you played and how long it took. You can write a note about how the practice went overall.

17

And it's all in the journal. Every session, every note, waiting for the next time you wonder what you were working on.
