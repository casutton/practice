# Organizing Your Tunes

**Why organize your tunes:** As your tune library grows, you'll want to find tunes quickly. Maybe you're looking for Irish tunes for a session, or you want to practice all your waltzes. Organizing your tunes helps you find what you need when you need it.

Practice! provides several ways to organize your tune library: traditions, tags, custom fields, and instruments. You don't need to use all of them - pick the ones that make sense for how you think about your music.

## Traditions

**Why use traditions:** A tradition tells you where a tune comes from - Old Time, Irish, Scottish, Cape Breton, Cajun, Quebecois, Bluegrass. It's useful when you want to work on tunes from one tradition, or you're getting ready for a session with a particular flavour. Traditions are simple: each tune has one, so filtering is easy.

### Managing Traditions

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Open Settings</strong>
    <p>Tap the gear icon in the bottom navigation bar.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Navigate to Tradition Settings</strong>
    <p>In the "Tune Organization" section, tap "Manage Traditions".</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <strong>Add a tradition</strong>
    <p>Tap the "+" button to add a new tradition and enter its name. There's also "Add Default Traditions" if you'd rather start from a ready-made list.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">4</span>
    <strong>Edit or delete traditions</strong>
    <p>Swipe left on a tradition to edit or delete it.</p>
</div>

### Assigning a Tradition to a Tune

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Open a tune</strong>
    <p>From the Tunes tab, tap on a tune to view its details.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Tap Edit</strong>
    <p>Tap the "Edit" button to modify the tune.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <strong>Select a tradition</strong>
    <p>Use the Tradition picker to assign one to the tune.</p>
</div>

## Tags

**Why use tags:** Tags are more flexible than traditions - a tune can have multiple tags, so you can organize tunes in different ways. Maybe you tag tunes by mood (happy, sad, energetic), by difficulty (beginner, intermediate, advanced), or by occasion (session tunes, performance pieces, learning tunes). Tags let you find tunes based on whatever criteria matter to you.

Tags provide flexible organization. A tune can have multiple tags, making it easy to find tunes by mood, difficulty, occasion, or any other category. Use tags however makes sense for your practice - there's no right or wrong way.

### Adding Tags to Tunes

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Open a tune</strong>
    <p>Navigate to the tune's detail view.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Tap Edit</strong>
    <p>Tap the "Edit" button.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <strong>Add tags</strong>
    <p>In the tags field, type a tag name and tap "Add". You can add multiple tags to a single tune.</p>
</div>

You can also manage the whole list from **Settings > Tune Organization > Manage Tags**.

<div class="info">
    <strong>One reserved word:</strong> <code>favorite</code> is used internally to mark favourited tunes, so it won't show up as a tag of your own. Use the star on the tune itself for that.
</div>

### Using Tags for Search

Tags can be used in advanced search to find tunes that match specific criteria.

## Custom Fields

**Why use custom fields:** Maybe you want to note which book a tune came from, who taught it to you, or the key you prefer it in. Custom fields let you keep anything that matters to you on the tune itself.

Fields belong to the tune, not to the library - you add them where you need them, and a field you only ever want on one tune costs nothing. Each one is a name and a value, both plain text.

### Adding a Custom Field

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Open a tune and tap "Edit"</strong>
    <p>From the Library, tap the tune you want, then Edit.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Find "Custom Info"</strong>
    <p>Scroll down to the Custom Info section.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <strong>Tap "Add Field"</strong>
    <p>Give the field a name and a value - "Learned from / Bruce Molsky", "Book / Portland Collection p.42", whatever is useful.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">4</span>
    <strong>Save</strong>
    <p>Tap "Save". The field shows up under Custom Info whenever you open the tune.</p>
</div>

<div class="tip">
    Custom fields are searchable - their values are matched by the library's search box along with titles and notes.
</div>

## Instruments

**Why track instruments:** If you play multiple instruments (like fiddle and mandolin), tracking which instrument you used helps you see how much you're practicing on each one. Maybe you want to make sure you're giving equal time to both instruments, or you want to see your progress on each one separately. Instrument tracking gives you insight into how you're dividing your practice time.

If you play multiple instruments, you can track which instrument you used for each practice session. This helps you see your practice patterns and make sure you're giving each instrument the attention it needs.

### Enabling Instrument Tracking

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Open Settings</strong>
    <p>Go to Settings > Practice Settings.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Enable instrument tracking</strong>
    <p>Toggle "Track Instruments" to enable.</p>
</div>

### Managing Instruments

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Open Instrument Settings</strong>
    <p>With instrument tracking enabled, tap "Manage Instruments" in Settings.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Add an instrument</strong>
    <p>Tap the "+" button to add a new instrument. Enter the instrument name and tap "Save".</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <strong>Edit or delete instruments</strong>
    <p>Swipe left on an instrument to edit or delete it.</p>
</div>

### Selecting an Instrument During Practice

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Start a practice session</strong>
    <p>When you start a practice session with instrument tracking enabled, you'll be prompted to select an instrument.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Choose an instrument</strong>
    <p>Select from your list of instruments, or enter a custom instrument name.</p>
</div>

## Searching and Filtering

### Basic Search

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Open the Library tab</strong>
    <p>Navigate to the Library tab.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Use the search bar</strong>
    <p>Library search casts wide on purpose. It matches the title, any <strong>alternate titles</strong>, the composer, the tradition, the type, your notes, tags, and the values of any custom fields - so searching "Molsky" finds the tunes you noted you learned from him.</p>
</div>

### Advanced Search

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">1</span>
    <strong>Open Advanced Search</strong>
    <p>In the Tunes tab, tap the search icon to open advanced search options.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">2</span>
    <strong>Apply filters</strong>
    <p>You can filter on name, tradition, type, key, tags, Comfort Level, favourites, and whether a tune is in This week or your Repertoire. Filters combine, so "Old Time reels I know" is one search.</p>
</div>

<div class="flow-step">
    <span class="step-number">3</span>
    <strong>Save searches (optional)</strong>
    <p>Save a search you keep reaching for and it joins the Saved Searches list. Repertoire and This week are built in and can't be deleted.</p>
</div>
